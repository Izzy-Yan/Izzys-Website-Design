The Complete Process

From quote to launch, here's exactly what to expect

1

Initial Contact & Quote

You can reach out via:

  • Email at [email protected]
  • Direct message on my Fiverr profile

What to include: Describe your project — what you need (new site, updates, design refresh), any specific features, your timeline, and your budget range if you have one.

Response time: I'll review your request and send a custom quote within 24 hours (usually much faster).

↓
2

Custom Fiverr Offer

Once we agree on scope and price, I'll send you a custom offer through Fiverr that outlines:

  • Exact deliverables (number of pages, features included, etc.)
  • Timeline and milestones
  • Total price and payment terms
  • What's included in revisions

Why Fiverr? This protects both of us — your payment is held securely by Fiverr until you approve the work, and I'm guaranteed payment once the project is complete.

↓
3

Secure Payment & Project Start

You accept the offer and place your order on Fiverr. Your payment is held in escrow — you don't pay me directly until you approve the final work.

What happens next:

  • I send you a detailed project intake form
  • You provide content, branding guidelines, and reference sites
  • Timeline and milestones are confirmed
  • Project officially begins!
↓
4

Design & Development

I build your website from start to finish. All communication happens through Fiverr's messaging system for transparency and protection.

Timeline depends on package:

  • Quick updates: 2-3 days
  • Starter websites: 5-7 days
  • Standard websites: 7-14 days
  • Premium websites: 14-21 days

You'll receive: Regular updates and previews as I work. You can see progress without waiting for the final product.

↓
5

First Draft Review

I deliver the complete first draft of your website for review. You'll get:

  • Full access to view the site on desktop and mobile
  • Testing links and forms
  • Time to review with your team

Your feedback: You submit one comprehensive list of changes — this is your included revision round. Be thorough!

↓
6

Revisions & Refinement

I implement all of your requested changes from the revision round. This includes:

  • Content updates (text, images)
  • Design tweaks (colors, fonts, layouts)
  • Functionality adjustments
  • Mobile responsiveness improvements

Timeline: Most revisions are completed within 2-3 days.

↓
7

Final Approval & Launch

Once revisions are complete, you review the final version. If everything looks perfect:

  • Your site is prepared for launch
  • Final testing across devices and browsers
  • Site goes live (or files delivered to you)
  • You approve the order on Fiverr

What you receive: Live website, all source files, and basic instructions for future updates.

↓
8

Post-Launch Support

After your site is live, I'm still available for:

  • Answering questions about your site
  • Quick bug fixes (within 7 days of launch)
  • Future updates and maintenance (billed separately)

Ongoing updates: Need changes down the road? Just message me on Fiverr and I'll send a new custom offer for the work.

Common Questions

What if I've never used Fiverr before?

No problem! Creating an account is free and takes 2 minutes. Once you're set up, I'll send you a custom offer and you can proceed from there. I'm happy to walk you through the process.

Can I pay outside of Fiverr?

Unfortunately no — I run all projects through Fiverr to keep both of us protected. It's safer for payments, provides clear project terms, and gives us both recourse if anything goes wrong.

What happens after the included revision round?

Any additional changes requested after your included revision round are billed separately. Small updates start at $25, and larger changes are quoted based on the work involved.

Do you provide hosting?

I can recommend hosting providers and help you get set up, but you'll purchase and manage your own hosting. This keeps costs low and gives you full control.

How long until my site is finished?

Timeline depends on your package: quick updates in 2-3 days, starter sites in 5-7 days, standard sites in 1-2 weeks, and premium sites in 2-3 weeks. Rush delivery may be available for an additional fee.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

Your satisfaction is guaranteed through Fiverr's buyer protection. I work with you through revisions to get everything right. In the rare case we can't reach an agreement, Fiverr's resolution center can help.
